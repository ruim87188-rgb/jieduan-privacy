Privacy Policy — App Curfew

Last updated: July 2026

Thank you for using App Curfew ("the App"). We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how the App handles your data.

1. We do not collect your personal data

The App requires no sign-up or login and has no account system. All data generated while using the App — including your blocked-app list, focus schedule, delay-time settings, the personal quit-reason text you write yourself, and intervention event records of your "continue" and "leave" choices (used for cooldown timing and daily-usage counts) — is stored locally on your device only, using Android's Room database and DataStore. Nothing is uploaded to any server or shared with third parties.

2. About the "Usage Access" permission

The App requires the Usage Access permission to identify which app is currently in the foreground, so it can determine whether to trigger a reminder for a blocked app during your configured focus period.

  • Usage data obtained through this permission is used only in real time for this on-device decision; it is never logged, stored, or uploaded.
  • The App never sends your app-usage habits to us or to any third party.

3. About the "Display over other apps" (overlay) permission

The App requires the overlay permission to display its own reason-based reminder screen during focus periods — showing the personal quit-reason text you wrote yourself, full-screen in front of a blocked app so you can pause and reconsider. This permission is used solely to present the App's own reminder UI — never for ads or data collection.

4. Data deletion

When you uninstall the App, all local data described above is automatically removed by the system. We do not keep any server-side copy, since the App has no backend server at all.

5. Third-party services

The App does not integrate any third-party advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or tracking tool.

6. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy is updated, the new version will be posted on this page along with a revised "Last updated" date.

8. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via the contact information listed on the App's Google Play store listing.
